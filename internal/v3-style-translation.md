# Avery Chen V3 — 3D Style Translation

## Creative intent

Revamp Avery as an original, stylized-realistic, early-career federal program analyst: energetic enough to hold a Gen Z audience, composed enough to communicate competence and nonpartisan public service. The reference contributes broad visual principles—an assertive top-heavy silhouette, warm expressive face, dark navy utility layers, tapered trousers, vivid magenta/teal accents, and lively explanatory poses—not a character likeness, uniform, logo system, or credential design.

The source is a compressed 480 × 360 JPEG, so tiny text and exact source swatches are not reliable production data. The values below are the V3 lock; do not eyedrop the JPEG.

**One-line read:** sculptural textured updo → open bespectacled face → cropped navy utility shape over a light center → high waist and tapered cuffs → grounded low-top sneakers, with magenta used as a controlled pulse rather than a brand color.

## 1. Silhouette and proportions

### Proportion lock

- Model at real scale: **1.67 m sole to skull crown**, approximately **1.75 m to the tallest curl** in the neutral groom.
- Use **7.25 head heights** sole-to-crown. The head is deliberately about 6–8% larger than naturalistic adult proportion, but Avery must not read as chibi or mascot-like.
- Head block: about **230 mm crown-to-chin**, **145 mm cheekbone width**, with a fully developed back of skull. Do not flatten the cranium to make room for the hair.
- Shoulder breadth: **375–390 mm**. Keep visible clavicle, rib-cage, waist, and pelvis masses beneath clothing; no wasp waist.
- Crotch-to-floor is about **47% of sole-to-crown height**. Legs read long because of the high waist and taper, not because knees or femurs are displaced.
- Hands are slightly graphic but anatomically credible: about **175 mm wrist crease to middle fingertip**. Feet are about **245 mm heel to toe** before sneaker volume.
- Neutral anatomy checks: elbows meet the natural waist/lower ribs; wrists meet the upper hip; fingertips reach mid-thigh; knees face the same direction as toes; the neck supports the skull instead of disappearing into the jacket.

### Shape language

- Primary shapes are **rounded and buoyant** in hair, cheeks, shoulders, and sneaker toes.
- Secondary shapes are **clean and tapered** in jacket seams, trouser legs, glasses, and accessories.
- Avoid both extremes: no rubber-hose limbs or giant doll head, and no photoreal body with a cartoon face pasted onto it.
- Maintain slight human asymmetry: hair peak off-center, one brow 1–2 mm higher at rest, jacket hem and rolled cuffs not perfectly mirrored. Bone placement and eye alignment remain stable.

### Thumbnail read

At 128 px character height, the silhouette must still show:

1. an asymmetric high textured hair mass;
2. a distinct head/neck gap and jacket shoulder line;
3. open jacket framing a light shirt;
4. a clear high waist;
5. tapered trousers terminating in visible cuffs;
6. low, substantial sneakers.

## 2. Face, skin, and glasses

### Head and skin construction

- Give Avery an original **soft-square/oval** head: fuller upper cheeks, gently squared jaw corners, and a rounded—not pointed—chin. This departs from the reference’s narrower heart-shaped face.
- Build actual brow ridge, eye sockets, cheek pads, muzzle/oral cylinder, jaw hinge, and chin projection. In profile, the brow, nose, lips, and chin must occupy separate depth planes.
- Skin reads medium-warm brown with a copper-neutral undertone. Use subtle hue variation rather than painted contouring: warmer ears, nose tip, lips, and upper cheeks; slightly cooler jaw/temples.
- Cheek fullness sits below and slightly outside the eyes. Smile deformation should lift this volume and create a modest lower-lid compression without inflating the whole face.
- Keep pores and micro-normal restrained. At a head-and-shoulders camera distance, Avery should read as a crafted animated person, not scanned skin or vinyl.

### Eyes and lids

- Use large almond eye openings, but keep globe size and socket placement plausible. Eye opening width is approximately **27–29 mm at model scale**; the intercanthal gap is roughly one eye-opening width.
- Sclera is warm off-white, never pure white. Iris is dark umber with a still-visible near-black pupil and a soft limbal ring.
- At rest, both iris centers are on the same horizontal line within **0.5 mm model-space tolerance**. Pupils remain centered in their irises. Aim both eyes at a common point approximately **2 m in front of the face** for the default camera look; do not leave them divergent, independently rotated, or cross-eyed.
- The upper lid naturally overlaps the top **8–12% of the visible iris** at neutral. The lower lid touches the globe and just kisses the lower iris; there must be no gap, floating lid edge, or full ring of sclera.
- Blinks close through lid rotation and compression over the globe, not by vertically scaling flat shapes. Preserve lid thickness and a clean inner/outer canthus.
- Lashes are short tapered curves or narrow volumetric meshes. Use one readable upper lash rhythm, not a dense cosmetic fan.

### Brows

- Brows are dark, full, softly angled, and made from textured curves/mesh rather than a flat decal.
- Neutral inner brow sits slightly lower than the arch, producing an attentive, friendly rise of about **6–8°**. Avoid permanently worried inner brows or sharply villainous arches.
- Rig inner, middle, and outer regions independently for subtle emphasis. Neutral asymmetry may be slight; expression poses must not make one brow appear detached.

### Nose, cheeks, lips, and ears

- Nose is a compact three-dimensional wedge with a soft bridge, rounded tip, real alar volume, nostril rims, and a defined columella. Keep nostrils dark through occlusion, not black paint.
- The nose projects clearly in side and 3/4 views. Do not reduce it to a front-facing button or texture mark.
- Lips are full but controlled: clear cupid’s bow, softly fuller lower lip, closed corners at neutral, and a proper oral cavity behind them. Lip color is a deep muted berry integrated with the skin, not glossy magenta lipstick.
- Neutral mouth is fully closed with **all teeth hidden**. Preserve a soft lip seal rather than an inked black line.
- Ears sit approximately between brow and nose-base height, with simplified but real helix, antihelix, concha, tragus, lobe, and rear attachment. They need enough depth to support glasses temples and to read in profile.

### Glasses

- Glasses are part of Avery’s standard V3 identity, not an occasional “smart” prop.
- Use an original **rounded trapezoid** frame: slightly wider at the brow, softened lower corners, thin deep-plum/navy acetate, and a narrow keyhole bridge. Lens width about **48 mm**, bridge **18 mm**, temple **138 mm** at model scale.
- The frame follows the face without intersecting lashes, brows, cheeks, ears, or hair. Temples have real thickness and curve behind the ears.
- Lenses are clear and anti-reflective. Reflections may describe the lens edge but may not obscure pupils in the approved portrait camera.

## 3. Dental and facial-performance standards

### Teeth and mouth interior

- Build a continuous, shallow **U-shaped upper dental arcade**. Individual teeth may be modeled, but contacts must be close; visible gaps at the front should be less than **0.2 mm** at real scale.
- Upper central incisors lead the arc, with gently reduced lateral incisors and canines. Avoid identical “piano key” rectangles, isolated floating cubes, an unnaturally flat row, or a single featureless white slab.
- Use off-white enamel, warmer toward the gumline. No pure-white or emissive teeth. Gums and tongue are muted warm rose and remain secondary.
- A friendly open smile normally reveals the upper **6–8 teeth** as one coherent arc. Lower teeth are only a restrained edge when articulation requires them.
- Surprise may expose the upper arc and tongue but never two full rows of teeth. Mouth corners and jaw hinge must support the opening.

### Required expression set

1. **Attentive neutral:** relaxed jaw, sealed lips, teeth hidden, aligned gaze, upper lid resting naturally on the iris, friendly brows.
2. **Approachable smile:** cheeks rise, outer eye corners soften, mouth corners move up and back, upper dental arc visible only after the lips separate.
3. **Focused/analytical:** 5–8% lid narrowing, small brow convergence, lips gently pressed or minimally parted; do not turn competence into suspicion.
4. **Encouraging/explaining:** one brow raised slightly, small head tilt, warm closed smile or light upper-tooth reveal.
5. **Surprised/problem-solving:** upper lids open only about 10–12% beyond neutral, brows rise without becoming circular, jaw drops into a rounded “oh”; read as discovery, not alarm.

### Viseme minimum

- **M/B/P:** complete soft lip seal; no teeth.
- **F/V:** upper incisors contact the inside of the lower lip without penetrating it.
- **A/AA:** jaw opening led by the hinge, soft vertical oval, upper teeth optional.
- **E/I:** lateral lip spread with restrained upper dental reveal; do not stretch cheeks into a grimace.
- **O/U/W/Q:** genuine lip rounding and forward projection, not a flat circular blendshape.
- **L:** tongue tip reaches behind the upper incisors.
- **TH:** tongue passes lightly between the incisors with no clipping.
- Blendshapes must combine with jaw, cheek, lid, and brow motion. Test every viseme with glasses on and at the approved portrait focal length.

## 4. Hair: a true 360° textured updo

### Original construction

- Base color is near-black espresso. Construct **six to eight flat-twist or braid channels** sweeping diagonally from the left temple and nape toward a high right-rear gathering point.
- Resolve the crown into an irregular three-lobed updo made from **eight to ten chunky rope braids/coil bundles**, with selected curled ends. This gives the reference’s confident textured volume while changing its topology and center of gravity.
- Add two restrained temple coils and one or two nape coils. Loose strands should frame rather than obscure the eyes.
- Magenta appears as woven thread, inner-braid streaks, and a few coil tips. Keep it to approximately **8–12% of visible hair area**; never turn the updo into a solid pink mass.

### Silhouette by view

- **Front:** peak extends about **80–90 mm above the skull crown**, shifted roughly **20 mm to Avery’s right**. Maximum hair width is about **1.30× face width**.
- **3/4:** show clear parallax between front coils, braided base, crown bundle, and rear coils. The hairstyle must not collapse into one spherical blob.
- **Side:** preserve forehead, occiput, and nape anatomy. The updo projects approximately **60–70 mm behind the skull** and has a visible undercut of negative space above the rear neck.
- **Back:** braid paths converge plausibly into the gathering point. Use a rounded triangular/three-lobed outline, not a perfect bun circle. Show the nape coils and the jacket collar beneath it.

### Geometry and shader

- Major braids, coils, hairline, brows, and silhouette flyaways must be volumetric curves or meshes with radial depth. **Do not use camera-facing hair cards, painted silhouette planes, or a flat back cap.**
- Each hero braid needs a readable helical or interlocked rhythm in close-up, but aggregate into broader clumps at normal camera distance. Avoid noisy “every strand” geometry.
- Use groom variation in thickness, twist, and roughness; keep the outer contour intentional. A small secondary-motion rig should lag the large crown mass subtly while temple/nape coils remain controlled.

## 5. Wardrobe and accessories

### Navy utility jacket

- Cut: soft-structured, waist-length utility/bomber hybrid with a shallow stand collar, open front, slightly rounded dropped shoulder, and hem **20–30 mm below the trouser waistband**. It must read professional-casual, not tactical or military.
- Sleeves are pushed or rolled to the high forearm with asymmetrical but plausible folds. Include a narrow magenta inner-cuff flash.
- Use two low-profile angled welt pockets, a back yoke, articulated elbow seam, and restrained topstitching. No epaulettes, rank tabs, medal bars, flag patch, chest armor, or bulky cargo pockets.
- Jacket thickness, collar roll, cuff layers, zipper tape, and seam piping are modeled. Cloth must sit over the torso rather than being painted onto it.

### Light shirt

- Warm soft-white crew-neck knit, tucked cleanly into the trousers.
- Give the collar and sleeve edges thickness, subtle ribbing, and soft compression under the lanyard. No slogan, logo, microscopic pattern, or pure-white blowout.

### High-waisted cuffed trousers

- Deep blue-charcoal utility twill, high waist at the natural waist, gentle front pleat, slanted front pockets, rear welt pockets, and one clean side seam.
- Leg is relaxed through the thigh, articulated at the knee, and tapered to a **35–40 mm-deep cuff** above the ankle. Do not use skin-tight leggings or oversized military cargo pockets.
- Folds follow body mechanics: compression at hip and knee, tension from crotch to raised knee, and controlled stacking above the cuff. Keep the back rise anatomically plausible.

### Belt and magenta accents

- **28–30 mm** charcoal woven belt with a compact rectangular gunmetal buckle and one magenta keeper. No badge-like buckle.
- Magenta is limited to the hair weave, jacket inner cuff/zip pull, belt keeper, sock stripe, and sneaker heel tab. Target **4–6% of the full-body visible surface area**.

### Socks and sneakers

- Socks are ribbed deep navy with one narrow magenta stripe and one thinner teal stripe. Show **20–30 mm** above the sneaker in neutral stance.
- Sneakers are original low-top court/running hybrids: navy textile upper, warm-white rubber cupsole, teal piping, magenta heel pull, and a charcoal outsole.
- Use real toe-box, tongue, lace, eyelet, heel-counter, sole, and tread volumes. No recognizable commercial silhouette, swoosh, stripes, or copied tread.

### Lanyard and badge

- Lanyard is woven teal/navy with a magenta safety-breakaway tab and a small gunmetal clip. It follows gravity, clears the collar, and has a simple secondary rig.
- Badge is a rounded vertical card approximately **54 × 86 × 1.2 mm**, hanging around lower sternum level. Use an abstract portrait block, three unreadable/placeholder information bars, and the V3 spark motif as a faint watermark.
- Do not write an agency name, “U.S.,” “federal government,” a department acronym, or a real credential class. Do not imitate PIV/CAC layout, federal seal placement, holograms, barcodes, or access-level colors.
- Badge laminate is anti-glare; it may catch an edge highlight but may not become the brightest object in frame.

### Original jacket patches

Use one agency-neutral graphic family:

- Motif: a single **four-point spark** between two short tapered chevrons. It may suggest motion or wings, but must not form a bird, eagle, shield, crest, or circular seal.
- Left chest: **32 × 24 mm** rounded woven patch with the small spark and two bars, no readable words.
- Right upper sleeve: **48 × 18 mm** patch with three staggered teal/cyan/magenta bars and three stitch dots.
- Back yoke: optional **90 mm-wide** low-contrast embroidery of the same spark/chevrons, tone-on-tone navy with one short magenta stitch. Keep it subordinate to the hair.
- Embroidery has 0.3–0.5 mm relief and follows garment curvature; it is not a floating card.

## 8. Camera and turntable translation

### Standard camera test

- Blender color management: **AgX**, look **Medium High Contrast**, exposure **0.0**, gamma **1.0**, sRGB display.
- Full-body approval camera: **70 mm full-frame equivalent**, camera near solar-plexus height, enough distance to avoid head/foot perspective exaggeration.
- Portrait approval camera: **85 mm full-frame equivalent**, lens center at eye height.
- Neutral test background: `#C7CDD1`, matte.
- Key: large soft source at camera-left 35–45° and 25–35° above eye line, nominal **5600 K**.
- Fill: broad frontal source at **0.40× key**.
- Rim: soft source behind camera-right at **0.25× key**, nominal **6200 K**. It should separate dark hair from navy without drawing a neon outline.
- Render full body and bust at yaw **0° front, 35° 3/4, 90° side, and 180° back**. Add a slow 360° turntable before approval.

### Camera acceptance bands

After exposure is set from an 18% gray reference, inspect diffuse mid-lit areas on a waveform:

- hair: **5–12 IRE** while retaining braid separation;
- jacket: **12–22 IRE** with seam and fold detail;
- trousers: **16–26 IRE**, visibly distinct from jacket;
- skin: **42–58 IRE**, with neither red-channel clipping nor gray/ashy shadows;
- shirt/sole: **70–84 IRE**, never clipped;
- magenta: **28–45 IRE**, saturated but non-emissive.

Reject the palette/material pass if pupils disappear into irises at portrait scale, lenses hide either pupil, navy collapses to black, shirt exceeds 90 IRE, skin turns orange under the key, or magenta clips into a flat red/pink patch.

### Required 3D read by angle

- **Front:** facial centerline, aligned eyes, glasses fit, open-jacket framing, high waist, lanyard gravity, and trouser taper are immediately legible.
- **3/4:** cheek, muzzle, nose, lens, ear, hair layers, lapel/collar, and lanyard must show real parallax. No feature may behave like a camera-facing sticker.
- **Side:** retain forehead-to-nose-to-lip-to-chin profile, full cranium, ear depth, glasses temple, braid convergence, chest/rib/pelvis masses, buttock-to-heel line, cuff thickness, and sneaker toe/heel volume.
- **Back:** show braid routing and gathering point, nape curls, collar/yoke layering, shoulder-blade and elbow folds, trouser darts/pockets, back rise, cuff overlap, heel counters, and tread.
- Flat cards are not acceptable for hair masses, lashes, brows, glasses, lanyard, badge, patches, clothing panels, or facial features. Tiny non-silhouette fuzz may use render curves.

## 9. Expression and action-pose attitude

Take the reference’s buoyant competence, not its exact poses.

### Performance principles

- Avery’s energy comes from a clear line of action, eye contact, and purposeful hands—not extreme squash, broad mugging, or mascot behavior.
- Default posture is upright with relaxed shoulders, unlocked knees, and weight slightly favoring one leg. Keep the sternum open and the chin neutral.
- Gestures remain inclusive and explanatory: open palm, small counting gesture, presenting a tablet, or indicating a diagram. Avoid finger guns, superhero stance, hands-on-hips authority, salute, crossed-arm defensiveness, or exaggerated shrug.

### Pose recipes

1. **Welcome/explain:** weight 60/40 on the back leg, pelvis rotated 6°, torso counter-rotated 8°, one forearm open palm-up at lower-rib height, other hand near the lanyard or tablet. Friendly smile and direct gaze.
2. **Walk-and-talk:** lead foot planted, rear heel lifted, torso leaning only 4–6° into travel, one hand marking a point. Hair and lanyard lag subtly; jacket hem reacts to motion.
3. **Analyze:** tablet held around lower sternum, torso inclines 7–9°, gaze shifts to the screen while pupils remain mutually aligned, free hand makes a precise pinch/point. Focused mouth, not a scowl.
4. **Discovery:** weight shifts forward, free hand opens, head retracts 10–15 mm, brows rise, mouth forms a restrained rounded surprise. Read “I found the issue,” not panic.
5. **Encourage:** modest 5° head tilt, one open hand toward the viewer, soft closed smile or minimal upper-tooth reveal; no thumbs-up caricature.

Hands must retain palm volume, knuckles, nail beds, and believable finger arcs in every pose. Check lanyard, badge, cuff, glasses, hair, and trouser intersections after posing.

## 10. Originality and agency-neutral guardrails

### Do not copy from the reference

- The “PattyPatties” name, typography, star/wing logo, slogan, page graphics, or exact palette-strip presentation.
- “U.S. Federal Government Employee,” any exact “US” garment marks, or similar letter placement.
- The reference’s circular tablet seal, ID design, sleeve lettering, back emblem, exact wing/eagle-like insignia, or exact patch arrangement.
- The source character’s facial likeness, heart-shaped head, exact high-puff topology, curl placement, beauty details, expression drawings, action-pose composition, or translucent interface graphics.
- Any real federal seal, agency logo, eagle, flag, shield, ring of stars, department acronym, campaign mark, political symbol, military insignia, law-enforcement badge, or commercial sneaker mark.

### What must remain neutral

- Avery is a **career civil servant and program analyst**, not political staff, military, law enforcement, intelligence, or a named-agency spokesperson.
- Clothing is professional utility casual, not a uniform. No tactical webbing, armor, rank, medals, holsters, or authority-signaling badge on the body.
- Badge and patches are decorative production-world identifiers only; they must not imply access authority or recreate government credentials.
- The spark/chevron motif uses one four-point spark, no enclosing circle, no bird anatomy, no shield, no red-white-blue grouping, and no readable agency name.
- Expressions communicate competence, care, curiosity, and clear service. Avoid partisan bravado, cynical “bureaucrat” comedy, childish mascot energy, or influencer swagger.
